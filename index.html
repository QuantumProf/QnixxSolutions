FAQ

Common questions, answered honestly.

Do you work with individuals or only registered businesses?

Both. Most of our engagements are with businesses and institutions, but we also take on individual projects where it's a good technical fit.

How does pricing work?

Project-based work is quoted per scope after an initial consultation, typically ₦150,000 to ₦3,000,000 depending on complexity. We require a 40–50% deposit before starting, with the balance tied to milestones.

Do you work with clients outside Ilorin?

Yes. Most engineering and design work happens remotely regardless of location; on-site installation and support is arranged case by case depending on the project.

What's your typical turnaround time?

It depends on the tier — a design consultation can take 2–4 weeks, a full prototype build 6–10 weeks. We give you a specific timeline before any work begins, not after.

Can you repair a device you didn't originally build?

In most cases, yes. We charge a diagnostic fee upfront regardless of outcome, since some devices are beyond an economical repair — we'll tell you honestly before committing to a fix.

Who can join the training programme?

University students and secondary school leavers with an interest in electronics or software — no prior embedded systems experience is required for the introductory cohort.
