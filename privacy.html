Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

QNIXX SOLUTIONS ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it, in line with Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

What We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message content you provide. If you enrol in a training cohort, purchase a product, or engage us for a project, we collect the additional information necessary to deliver that service, such as billing and delivery details.

Where our hardware products are deployed — for example, a wearable health device or a smart energy meter — the device may collect operational data (such as sensor readings, location, or usage patterns) as part of its core function. This data is covered separately in the terms provided at the point of purchase or deployment for that specific product.

How We Use It

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To deliver services you've engaged us for, including project work, training, and repairs
  • To maintain records required for business, tax, and regulatory compliance
  • To improve our products and services based on real usage and feedback

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information outside QNIXX SOLUTIONS except where required by law, or where a service provider (such as a cloud hosting platform) needs it strictly to help us deliver our service to you.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact Us
